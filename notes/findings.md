plus the evidence pointer. A claim is only `SUPPORTED` with ≥3 seeds + variance + a baseline
(per the A* bar in `CLAUDE.md`). Negative results are logged here too — they are valuable.

## Open threads / risks
- F3 (the `~d/n_d` sub-critical law) is **refuted** (R²=0.18); we no longer claim a clean
  closed-form for the transition. The load-bearing scaling claim is F2 (d* grows with n_d),
  which holds. The allocation law (C3) only needs "a modest d* exists" — it does.
- C4's escape is now scoped to the free-vector/worst-case regime (F18: it does not transfer to
  real encoders). The paper's spine is C1+C2+C3; C4 is a scoped "when does multi-view help?"
  section, not the headline. This protects C2 and avoids overclaiming the method.
- Whether **adversarial** inputs can push real ρ negative (super-multiplicative) is the one
  genuinely open question worth flagging in the paper — E7 found ρ≈0 on a benign corpus; LIMIT
  shows retrieval can be driven to ~0, but the *joint* ρ under adversarial pressure is untested.
- Watch for anyone publishing F4/F5 first — see `related-work.md`.
